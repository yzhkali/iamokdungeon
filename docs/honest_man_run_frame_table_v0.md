## Timing

- Suggested GIF frame duration: 80-95 ms per frame.
- Contact and down frames may feel slightly heavier.
- Do not hold any frame longer in the first version. Test even timing first.

## Common Failure Checks

- If feet slide, reduce horizontal foot travel and strengthen contact poses.
- If it feels like a walk, increase flight height and arm swing.
- If it feels silly, reduce head bob and limb spread.
- If it feels weightless, deepen frames 2 and 6.
- If the belly disappears, widen torso silhouette in every frame.
- If the open shirt looks pasted on, delay shirt flap motion by one frame.

## Acceptance Criteria

The animation passes v0 only if:

- Frames 1 and 5 clearly show opposite-foot contact.
- Frames 2 and 6 clearly show weight compression.
- Frames 4 and 8 clearly show light flight / transition.
- The head remains stable enough that the character feels controlled.
- The belly and shirt give the run weight.
- The loop reads within 2 seconds of watching it.
